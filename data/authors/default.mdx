Luke Bechtel is a creative technologist, using tech to make a positive impact on people. He is currently a Founding Engineer at [Infinity](https://infinity.inc), building AI inference infrastructure — production LLM serving on state-of-the-art GPUs (H100, B200), CUDA kernel optimization, and agentic systems that optimize other systems. Before Infinity, he was a 2x technical founder ([Reasonote](https://www.reasonote.com), [Collider](https://www.collidertech.com)) and Director of Engineering at [Revaly](https://www.revaly.com). He has a full-stack background, including industrial automation, neural network research, and 3D printing, and has contributed to open-source projects like [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm), [Langchain](https://github.com/hwchase17/langchainjs), and [Trimesh](https://github.com/mikedh/trimesh). Also a lover of sci-fi, history, writing, and music production. Learn more about him by visiting his twitter, github, and linkedin (in that order :)
